# The footage prompts

The tiles use eight clips made with Veo 3.1 Fast (9:16, 720p, 4 seconds, no audio needed). The third b-roll tile is the first
b-roll clip mirrored and zoomed, because the generator rejected the last prompt.

Every prompt ends with the same guard so the clips carry no text or logos: "no text, no logos".

| Tile | Prompt |
| --- | --- |
| a1 | Vertical handheld smartphone footage, medium shot of a bearded man in a black cap speaking directly to the camera in a dim home office with a warm lamp and a wooden slat wall behind him, natural hand gestures, shallow depth of field, raw unedited look, no text, no logos, no subtitles. |
| a2 | Vertical handheld smartphone footage, medium close-up of a woman in her thirties speaking directly to the camera at a bright desk by a large window, natural hand gestures, soft daylight, shallow depth of field, raw unedited look, no text, no logos, no subtitles. |
| a3 | Vertical handheld smartphone footage, medium shot of a man in a denim jacket speaking to the camera on a city rooftop at golden hour, natural hand gestures, warm sunlight, shallow depth of field, raw unedited look, no text, no logos, no subtitles. |
| b1 | Vertical close-up B-roll, hands typing on a laptop keyboard at a dark desk at night, warm lamp light and soft screen glow on the fingers, shallow depth of field, slow subtle camera drift, cinematic, the screen is out of focus and shows no readable text, no logos. |
| b2 | Vertical close-up B-roll, a ceramic coffee cup being set down on a wooden desk beside an open notebook and a pen, soft morning light, slow subtle camera drift, shallow depth of field, cinematic, no text, no logos. |
| c1 | Vertical raw handheld phone footage walking through a busy city street at dusk, slightly shaky, warm shop lights and passing people out of focus, unedited look, natural colours, no text, no signs with readable words, no logos. |
| c2 | Vertical raw handheld phone footage walking down a modern office hallway with glass walls and people at desks out of focus, slightly shaky, natural colours, unedited look, no text, no signs with readable words, no logos. |
| c3 | Vertical raw handheld phone footage inside a busy cafe, people talking at wooden tables out of focus, warm window light, slightly shaky, natural colours, unedited look, no text, no signs with readable words, no logos. |

## Tips from the run

- Ask for "raw unedited look" and "slightly shaky" for the raw footage group, and "shallow depth of field, cinematic" for b-roll, so the three groups look different at 60 pixels wide.
- A prompt about a hand scrolling a phone was rejected by the generator's content filter. Rewording to a different subject worked.
- Check the generator's terms before commercial use of the clips. This kit does not ship them.
